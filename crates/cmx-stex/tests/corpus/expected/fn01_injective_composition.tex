Let $f : A \to B$ and $g : B \to C$ be injective functions.
We show that $g \circ f$ is injective.
Let $x, y \in A$ with $(g \circ f)(x) = (g \circ f)(y)$.
Then $g(f(x)) = g(f(y))$.
Since $g$ is injective, $f(x) = f(y)$.
Since $f$ is injective, $x = y$.
Hence $g \circ f$ is injective, as required. \hfill$\square$
