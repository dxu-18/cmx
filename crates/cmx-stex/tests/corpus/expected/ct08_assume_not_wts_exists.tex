We show that there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $n^{2} > 50$.
Assume for the sake of contradiction that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n^{2} \leq 50$.
Then $n^{2} \leq 50$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, but $8^{2} = 64 > 50$.
This is a contradiction.
Hence there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $n^{2} > 50$, as required. \hfill$\square$
