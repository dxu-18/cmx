We show that there is no $x \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $2x = 1$.
Assume for the sake of contradiction that there exists $x \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $2x = 1$.
Then $2x$ is even, but $1$ is odd.
This is a contradiction.
Hence there is no $x \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $2x = 1$, as required. \hfill$\square$
