Let $p$ be a prime.
We show that $p = 2$ or $p$ is odd.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Case 1: Suppose $p = 2$.
    Then the claim holds trivially.

    There is nothing more to show here.
  \item Case 2: Suppose $p \neq 2$.
    Suppose that $p$ is even.
    Then $2 \mid p$, and since $p$ is prime, $p = 2$, a contradiction.

    Hence $p$ is odd.
\end{itemize}

In either case, $p = 2$ or $p$ is odd. \hfill$\square$
