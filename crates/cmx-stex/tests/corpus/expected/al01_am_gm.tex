Let $a, b$ be nonnegative real numbers.
We show that $\frac{a + b}{2} \geq \sqrt{ab}$.
Note that $(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b})^{2} \geq 0$.
Expanding, $a - 2\sqrt{ab} + b \geq 0$, so $a + b \geq 2\sqrt{ab}$.
Dividing by 2 gives the claim.
This is the AM-GM inequality.
Hence $\frac{a + b}{2} \geq \sqrt{ab}$, as required. \hfill$\square$
