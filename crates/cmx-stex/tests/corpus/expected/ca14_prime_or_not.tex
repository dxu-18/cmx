Let $n$ be an integer with $n \geq 2$.
We show that $n$ has a prime divisor.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Case 1: Suppose $n$ is prime.
    Then $n$ is a prime divisor of $n$.
  \item Case 2: Suppose $n$ is not prime.
    Then $n = ab$ for some integers $a$, $b$ with $1 < a < n$ and $1 < b < n$, and by induction $a$ has a prime divisor $p$, which also divides $n$.
\end{itemize}

These cases are exhaustive, because every integer is either prime or not.
In all exhaustive cases, $n$ has a prime divisor. \hfill$\square$
