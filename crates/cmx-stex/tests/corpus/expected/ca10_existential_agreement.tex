Let $m, n$ be odd integers.
Then there exists $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $m = 2k + 1$.
There exists $l \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $n = 2l + 1$.
There exist integers $a$, $b$ such that $m = 2a + 1$ and $n = 2b + 1$.
There exists $j \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $j > n$.
There is no integer $x$ such that $x^{2} = 2$.
Therefore $m + n = 2(a + b + 1)$ is even.
