We count the 4-digit PINs with no repeated digit.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Stage 1: Pick the first digit in 10 ways.
  \item Stage 2: Pick the second digit in 9 ways.
  \item Stage 3: Pick the third digit in 8 ways.
  \item Stage 4: Pick the fourth digit in 7 ways.
\end{itemize}

Altogether there are $10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 = 5040$ such PINs.
