Let $P, Q$ be statements.
Then $P \oplus Q$ holds exactly when $P$ if and only if $\neg Q$.
In symbols $P \oplus Q \iff (P \lor Q) \land \neg (P \land Q)$.
Also $P \uparrow Q \iff \neg (P \land Q)$ and $P \downarrow Q \iff \neg (P \lor Q)$.
With the short forms, $P \oplus Q$, $P \uparrow Q$ and $P \downarrow Q$ mean the same.
