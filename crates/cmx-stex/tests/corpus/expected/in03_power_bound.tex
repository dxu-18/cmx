\textit{Theorem.} For every $n \geq 1$, $2^{n} \geq n + 1$.

\textit{Proof.} We proceed by induction on $n$.

\textit{Base case} ($n = 1$). $2^{1} = 2 \geq 2$.

\textit{Inductive step.} Suppose that $2^{k} \geq k + 1$ for some $k \geq 1$.
We show that $2^{k + 1} \geq k + 2$.
Then $2^{k + 1} = 2 \cdot 2^{k} \geq 2(k + 1)$ by the inductive hypothesis and $2(k + 1) = 2k + 2 \geq k + 2$.
Hence for every $n \geq 1$, $2^{n} \geq n + 1$, as required. \hfill$\square$
