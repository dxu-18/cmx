We show that $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} = 0$.
Let $\varepsilon > 0$ be arbitrary.
Choose $N \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N > \frac{1}{\varepsilon}$.
Then for every $n \geq N$, $\lvert \frac{1}{n} - 0 \rvert = \frac{1}{n} \leq \frac{1}{N} < \varepsilon$.
Hence $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} = 0$, as required. \hfill$\square$
