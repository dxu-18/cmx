Let $a, b, d \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $d \neq 0$.
Suppose that $d \mid a$ and $d \mid b$.
We show that $d \mid a + b$ and $d \mid a - b$.
There exist integers $m$, $n$ such that $a = dm$ and $b = dn$.
It follows that $a + b = d(m + n)$ and $a - b = d(m - n)$.
Consequently, $d \mid a + b$ and $d \mid a - b$. \hfill$\square$
