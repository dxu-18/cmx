\textit{Lemma.} Let $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Z}$.
If $a \mid b$ and $b \mid c$, then $a \mid c$.

\textit{Proof.} Suppose that $a \mid b$ and $b \mid c$.
Then there exist integers $k$ and $l$ such that $b = ak$ and $c = bl$.
Therefore $c = (ak)l = a(kl)$.
Since $kl \in \mathbb{Z}$, $a \mid c$.
Hence if $a \mid b$ and $b \mid c$, then $a \mid c$, as required. \hfill$\square$
